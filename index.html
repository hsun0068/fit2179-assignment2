FIT3179 Data Visualisation 2

Australia Health & Lifestyle

This interactive visualisation explores health and lifestyle patterns across Australia, focusing on state-level risk rankings, regional health outcomes, demographic differences, and national trends.

Project overview

This visualisation explores health and lifestyle patterns across Australia by comparing state-level risk rankings, regional health outcomes, demographic differences, and national trends. Through interactive charts, users can examine how lifestyle factors such as obesity, smoking, physical activity, alcohol risk, and mental health vary across Australian populations.

01

State lifestyle risk ranking

A clear opening view comparing the composite lifestyle risk index across states and territories.

02

Regional risk burden and health outcomes

Each bubble represents an Australian health region, with population shown by bubble size.

03

Interactive obesity and diabetes comparison

Use the controls to filter regions and compare obesity, diabetes and physical inactivity.

04

Obesity and diabetes gap by state

A lollipop chart compares two related health indicators for each state and territory.

05

National health trend explorer

Brush the timeline to focus on specific years and compare long-term national trends.

06

Coordinated regional risk events

A coordinated view linking regional risk event locations with a time-based trend.

07

Age and sex lifestyle heatmap

Select one indicator and compare rates across age groups and sex.

08

Healthy behaviour indicators

Grouped bars compare fruit, vegetable and physical activity indicators across states.

09

Lifestyle risk composition

Stacked bars show how different lifestyle factors contribute to each state’s overall risk profile.

Summary dashboard

A compact final summary of the key patterns across the visualisation.
